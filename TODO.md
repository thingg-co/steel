# Steel roadmap

Steel is Flint's architecture pointed at crypto. Below is the list of proposals for what to tackle
next, for the partners to prioritize (2026-10-11). Each is a hypothesis for the walk-forward
backtest where it touches the model: it ships behind a switch where it can, lands in one commit
with tests, and is judged by a backtest before and after. Items that change the feature set reset
the model once, so they are batched. Effort is a rough size: S (a day), M (a few days), L (a week+).

## M. Market Monkey parity and beyond (researched 2026-10-13)

MMT (mmt.gg, "Market Monkey Terminal") is a browser order-flow terminal: 20+ venues merged into one
feed, footprint/CVD/DOM/tape, order-book and liquidation heatmaps, Hyperliquid TP/SL/liquidation maps
from real positions, volume profiles and TPO, open interest/funding/liquidations/net positioning,
workspaces with linked widgets, alerts, bar replay, 19 drawing tools, on-chart execution on
Hyperliquid/Propr/Lighter with an algo suite, a typed scripting language with a 330-script community
catalog, a paid data API, and an MCP server. Steel already has what MMT does not: a forecasting model,
a paper book that pays every cost, a promotion checklist, a vault, identity resolution, the arbitrage
replay, the GPU handoff. The gap is the order-flow data layer and the chart. Each item below says what
MMT has, what Steel has, and the work. Order: the data layer first (M1 to M5), because every
indicator and heatmap is a view over it; then the chart (M6 to M9); then the derived views; then the
platform; then what goes beyond.
