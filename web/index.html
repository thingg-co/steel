    Candles are built from every live venue's prints (the tape) at any timeframe for the last day, and from the 5-minute bars beyond it. Wheel zooms, drag pans, double-click resets, ← → step. Zoom in to read the footprint: sells at the bid on the left of each candle, buys at the ask on the right, the point of control shaded, the 70% value area boxed, stacked imbalances in full color. Circles are large prints. The profile at the right edge is the visible range's volume at price (POC in yellow, the 70% value area brighter); TPO is the count of candles that touched each price; level lines are the day's open, high and low, yesterday's, the week's and the month's, from 5-minute closes; shift-click a candle to anchor a VWAP there. The book heatmap paints resting bids (blue) and asks (orange) over time; the liquidation map is modeled from open-interest changes and leverage bands, never real positions, with each cluster spent once price trades through it. Positioning counts only the flow open interest confirms (new longs, new shorts, closes); a yellow circle marks absorption, heavy new positioning that failed to move price. Triangles are liquidations (red below: longs, green above: shorts); arrows are the model's resolved calls, filled when they hit; the fan is its live forecast.
